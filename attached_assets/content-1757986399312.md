### Task App

Task App is a straightforward and efficient tool designed to help users manage their tasks effortlessly. This lightweight task tracker focuses on simplicity and functionality, making it an excellent choice for those who need a quick way to organize their work. Currently, the app operates without a database, storing tasks locally on the user's device. This approach ensures a seamless and fast experience, while also maintaining user privacy by keeping data offline. However, it's worth noting that this is a work in progress, with plans to expand its capabilities in the future.
