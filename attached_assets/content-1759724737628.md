About Plunderverse

"Plunderverse" is a 3D space outlaw game inspired by Firefly, built with React, Three.js, and Express. Set in a bankrupt solar system in 2149, players take on the roles of smugglers and space outlaws. The game centers around dynamic gameplay loops including trade and survival, mission and story progression, and combat and notoriety management, all within a procedurally generated, economically driven solar system. The goal is to deliver a comprehensive, highly replayable outlaw experience in space with a rich narrative and emergent gameplay.
